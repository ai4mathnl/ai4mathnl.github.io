About the workshop

The aim of the afternoon is not a series of results, but a direction. We want to arrive at a picture of where AI can genuinely advance mathematical research, where it cannot, and which questions the mathematical community in the Netherlands should be working on together over the coming years.

The format is deliberately discussion-heavy: a small number of talks to set the scene, followed by breakout sessions in which participants work out concrete elements of that vision, and a plenary session to bring the threads together.
