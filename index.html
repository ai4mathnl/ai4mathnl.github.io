About the workshop

We invite the mathematics community of the Netherlands to discuss what the future of mathematics with AI should look like.

We will continue the discussions started in, for example:

  • Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics A community initiative, endorsed by the International Mathematical Union
  • A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics Declaration signed by 25 Fields medallists, 11 September 2026
  • KWG Math & AI day Utrecht, 23 October 2026 — a closely related meeting of the Royal Dutch Mathematical Society

leading to a published document stating a shared vision of AIM and the broader Dutch mathematics community for the future of mathematics with AI.

Who should attend?

We encourage all interested mathematicians based in The Netherlands to attend, including PhD candidates, postdocs, independent researchers and senior academic staff. No expertise in AI or formalisation is required.

What will be discussed?

  • What does it mean to be a mathematician in the presence of AI?
  • Affirming the humanity of mathematics
  • Skill development & Training
  • Overhauling Publishing and Reviewing
  • Steering Politics
